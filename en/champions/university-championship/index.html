Champions of the All Japan University Championships by style and weight class, year by year. This page covers 1975–2025, 51 years. Click a name to open the wrestler's page.

Names are shown only for wrestlers who have a player page on this site, so that we never show a guessed spelling. Other names (mostly from earlier years) are shown as "—"; you can see them on the Japanese version of this page (日本語版).
