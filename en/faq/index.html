Frequently asked questions

What is this site?

The official archive of Japan Wrestling Channel. It organizes 3,360 Japanese wrestling streams and videos published on YouTube by tournament, year and day.

Where can I watch tournament streams?

Videos play right on this site in the YouTube player. Choose a tournament and year from the tournament list to see that edition's streams by day and mat.

Tournaments →

Can I see upcoming streams?

The tournament calendar shows upcoming tournaments by month. When stream slots are published, they appear on the tournament page as scheduled streams.

Calendar →

Are there videos of past tournaments?

Yes. Streams and videos from 66 tournaments since 2014 are listed. You can search by tournament name, nickname (e.g. Inter-College, Emperor's Cup) or year.

Search →

Can I find a wrestler's matches?

Yes. Player pages list each wrestler's tournaments, results and related videos.

Players →

Can I use the photos? I want a photo removed

Photos are published with the photographers' permission, and the copyright belongs to them. Please ask the photographer before using a photo. To request a removal, use the contact form and choose Photos (removal request).

Photo removal request →

How do I report a mistake or ask to list or hide a video?

Please use the contact form. We will review it and respond.

Contact →

How often is the site updated?

Every day at around 6:00 a.m. Japan time, new YouTube videos are added and the pages are rebuilt automatically.

Is there a Japanese version?

Yes. Every page has a Japanese version at the same address without /en/. English pages show tournament and venue names in English; video titles stay in Japanese as originally published.

Japanese version →

The official archive of Japan Wrestling Channel. It organizes Japanese wrestling videos published on YouTube (Japan Wrestling Channel and others) by tournament. Videos play right on this site in the YouTube player. Video titles are shown as originally published, in Japanese.

Dates, venues and sources come from our tournament reference data. Videos not yet confirmed to belong to a specific edition are marked "Pending review". English names of tournaments and venues are our own translations.
